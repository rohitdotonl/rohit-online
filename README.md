# The Product Design Course

A 21-guide course in product design for people who make software alone. It covers principles, craft and practice, plus breakdowns of physical objects, software and reading products. The guides include live demos, quizzes, exercises with rubrics, common mistakes and checklists.

Open `index.html` to start, or visit https://design.rohit.onl.

Progress, checklist ticks and theme are saved only in your browser.
