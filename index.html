By the end of the course you will be able to

  • Find a problem worth solving, and describe it so clearly that the design almost follows.
  • Structure a product: its objects, navigation, flows, states and words.
  • Make it look and feel right: hierarchy, type, colour, spacing, motion.
  • Make it work for everyone: accessible, honest, calm, fast.
  • Test it alone, learn from real use, and improve it on purpose.
  • Look at any product, physical or digital, and explain why it works.
  • Ship a complete product you designed end to end, and judge it against a professional rubric.

How this course works

You don't become a designer by reading. You become one by making many small decisions, seeing what they do, and getting a little better each week. The guides are built around that loop.

What you'll find in every guide

For the solo creator

Everything here assumes you are the researcher, designer, engineer, writer and support desk. There are no stakeholder workshops or hand-off documents. Where the classic advice assumes a team, the guides say what to do instead when it's just you.

The 24-week plan

About six to eight hours a week: two or three reading sessions, one practice block, and the weekly redesign. Run the capstone project alongside from week 6.
